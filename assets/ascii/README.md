`ink-garden.jpg` and `ink-garden.mp4` are the published media from the requested reference. CSS grayscale, blending, opacity, and edge masks match the website's light/dark themes. These files replace Creation of Adam only within the two subject folders; the home page retains its original artwork.

The lower `Garden` canvas adapts the independently authored renderer from the user-supplied `Study_Garden_Halftone_Garden_Corrected_V3.zip`. Its pointer spring and click ripple are retained, with a transparent canvas, theme colors, a fully fitted raised baseline, and the original footer's pause/reveal lifecycle. The original upstream TSX is not included.
